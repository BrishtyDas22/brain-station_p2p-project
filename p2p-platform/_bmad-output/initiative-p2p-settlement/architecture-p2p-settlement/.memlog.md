- (decision by architect) A1-A18 spine written. Load-bearing consequence of STRICT_TRANS_TABLES: money columns are BIGINT UNSIGNED, so a negative wallet is physically unrepresentable rather than merely forbidden
- (decision by architect) A7 resolves OQ-8 and OQ-9: the reversal owns its own transactions row; reversals.original_transaction_id carries a UNIQUE constraint as the once-only rule; REVERSED is derived from the existence of a reversals row, not from entry count; unique index uq_reversals_reversal_txn makes a-reversal-cannot-be-reversed a data-layer property
- (decision by architect) A7 keeps Invariant 1 intact with no exception clause: because the reversal is its own transaction, every transaction independently has exactly one DEBIT and one CREDIT
- (decision by architect) A8 resolves OQ-10: audit intent is an audit_outbox row written in the same transaction as the money (atomic with the money), drained asynchronously by an Audit Service worker into an independent immutable audit_log chain; the ledger never blocks on the audit sink
- (decision by architect) A11 resolves OQ-13: the chain-close transaction contains exactly the close-record insert plus the chain head advance; ACK strictly after commit; the POSTED status write is outside that ACID unit and after the ACK, so a crash leaves a chain-closed entry with a stale CHAIN_PENDING projection - status is a rebuildable projection and the ledger wins
- (decision by architect) A15 resolves OQ-11: the audit chain is a separate table in the same schema with its own genesis literal, its own canonicalization, its own audit_sequence_number, and trigger-protected append-only semantics
- (decision by architect) A3 account ownership: 1:1 wallet-to-ledger-account enforced by uq_wallets_account plus uq_ledger_accounts_owner; ledger_accounts.owner_user_id nullable and ck_ledger_accounts_type splits USER from SYSTEM so H-1 can be adopted later without a schema change
- (decision by architect) A5 finality: no status column on ledger_entries (adopting H-6's proposed-not-approved shape); append-only enforced by privilege revocation plus BEFORE UPDATE and BEFORE DELETE triggers; no cascades anywhere
- (decision by architect) A9 reservations: durable reservations rows are the source of truth, wallet.reserved_minor is a cache, and ck_wallets_reserved_within_balance is expressible because balance_minor and reserved_minor live on the same row; lifecycle HELD->POSTING->POSTED and HELD->RELEASED with fencing
- (decision by architect) A6 sequencing: per-account sequence allocated from ledger_accounts.next_sequence_number under row lock; accounts are always locked in ascending account_id order, never in transfer direction
- (decision by architect) A10 A13 A16 A17 A18: per-transaction lock_epoch fencing; idempotency key uniqueness per (user_id, idempotency_key) with a separate created_at and expires_at so M-4 tiered retention needs no schema change; chain closer is a singleton with leader lease plus startup assertion plus conditional update; partitioning deferred with only the scoped InnoDB FK-on-partitioned-tables limitation claimed; 'reconciliation partitioning by year' read as a scan filter not table partitioning
